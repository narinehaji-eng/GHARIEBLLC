Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated · July 2026

This policy explains how GHAREIB LLC ("we," "us") handles information in connection with our applications, including HabitFlow.

What we collect

Our apps are designed to run without an account or server backend. The habits, streaks, and completion history you enter are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android storage. We do not collect, receive, or have access to this information.

What we don't do

  • We do not require account creation or sign-in.
  • We do not transmit your habit data to us or to any third party.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with advertisers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs within HabitFlow.

Data on your device

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, permanently removes your saved habits and history, since nothing is kept anywhere else.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud backup — we'll update this page and the in-app version before the change takes effect.
